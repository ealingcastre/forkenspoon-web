Common questions

How do I keep a recipe private?

When adding or editing a dish, toggle on Secret Recipe. Secret recipes only ever show up in your own feed and recipe box — nobody else can see them.

How do I delete my account?

Open the profile menu → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, saved recipes list, and weekly planner. Recipes you've shared publicly stay up for the community but are re-attributed to "Deleted User" instead of your name.

How does the grocery list work?

Whatever you've added to your Weekly Planner is automatically rolled into your Grocery tab as a shopping list. You can check items off as you shop, or copy the whole list to your clipboard to paste anywhere.

What does "forking" a recipe mean?

Same idea as forking code — save your own editable copy of someone else's dish, tweak it however you like, and it stays linked back to the original so credit isn't lost.
