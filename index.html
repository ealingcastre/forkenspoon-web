The story, in a nutshell

Ideas from real home cooks

Browse what other families are actually cooking this week, not staged food-blogger shots. Save the ones you want to try, and watch the community's favourites naturally rise to the top.

Your recipe book

Grandma's lasagna, that Tuesday-night invention, the recipe your mate texted you — all saved with photos, ingredients, and steps. Keep the real secrets secret with Secret Recipe mode.

Fork a recipe

See a dish in the feed you're keen to try? Fork it, tweak it to taste, and it stays linked back to where the idea started.

Weekly planner

Drag your saved dishes onto the week ahead and just see it. No more "we have nothing planned" panic at 5pm.

Cook together

Set up a household so meal plans and grocery lists sync for everyone — no more "I thought YOU were sorting dinner" texts.

Grocery list, automatically

Whatever's on the plan turns into a shopping list — tick things off, or copy the whole thing to send to whoever's doing the shop.
